Netflix Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Netflix's vast media catalog. It uncovers trends across content releases, genres, age ratings, countries of origin, and content types (Movies vs. TV Shows) through rich visual storytelling and dynamic DAX metrics.

Project Overview

Designed and built an end-to-end Power BI report to investigate Netflix catalog growth and viewer segment preferences.

The project required extracting, cleaning, and transforming raw unstructured metadata into structured data models, followed by crafting intuitive UI dashboards with interactive slicers and drill-through capabilities.

Tools & Methods

Applied industry standard data transformation and BI tools:

Power BI Desktop DAX Calculations Power Query ETL Excel Modelling Data Cleansing Color Psychology & UX

Dashboard KPIs

  • Movies vs. TV Shows ratio distribution.
  • Top 10 producing countries and localized libraries.
  • Genre concentration by country and rating tier.
  • Maturity rating breakdowns (TV-MA, PG-13, R, TV-14).
  • Annual release timeline and post-2015 streaming boom.

Key Insights Discovered

  • Drama, Comedy, and International Movies represent over 60% of total titles.
  • The United States and India emerged as the largest content contributors.
  • TV-MA content experienced exponential growth starting from 2018 onwards.
  • The addition of new titles peaked significantly between 2018 and 2020.
